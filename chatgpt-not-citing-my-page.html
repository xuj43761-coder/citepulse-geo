Why ChatGPT Is Not Citing My Website: Rule Out These 3 Checks First

Updated November 2026 • 5-minute read • Quick GEO Answer

Short answer: Before chasing PR or community mentions, rule out three verifiable on-site blockers: (1) OAI-SearchBot is blocked in robots.txt or challenged by your CDN/WAF; (2) the page contains no extractable definitions, concrete numbers, or structured answers; or (3) your llms.txt and internal navigation point crawlers to thin, redirected, or empty URLs. Off-site authority and Reddit/forum mentions matter—but only after these three checks pass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is ChatGPT not citing my website even though it ranks on Google?

The most common technical causes are blocking OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt or a CDN firewall, rendering core facts only after client-side JavaScript execution, or publishing generic marketing prose without concrete definitions, numbers, or tables an answer engine can extract cleanly.

Should I focus on Reddit mentions or technical fixes first?

Always pass the three verifiable technical checks first: confirm OAI-SearchBot gets HTTP 200, make sure the page has citable definitions and numbers, and verify your llms.txt and sitemap point to live canonical URLs. Off-site community mentions help most after your own pages are crawlable and extractable.
