    Frequently Asked Questions
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?
          
    Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring web pages, entity definitions, verifiable facts, and machine-readable manifests like JSON-LD and llms.txt so AI search engines such as ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews accurately retrieve and cite your brand.
    
        
    
        
    
          Does CitePulse GEO send my HTML or proprietary copy to an external server?
          
    No. Every audit score, llms.txt manifest, and robots.txt policy is computed deterministically inside your local browser using client-side JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded or stored on any backend server.
    
        
    
        
    
          Should I block GPTBot while allowing OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt?
          
    For most SaaS companies and publishers, yes. Allowing OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot preserves live AI search citations and referral traffic, while disallowing GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and CCBot opts your content out of foundation model training.
