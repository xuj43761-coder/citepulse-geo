AI Crawlers robots.txt Matrix (2026): Search vs. Model Training

Updated November 2026 • 5-minute read • Technical GEO Reference

The biggest technical GEO mistake in 2026: Blanket-blocking every AI user-agent in robots.txt or a CDN firewall. Modern AI vendors split their crawlers into two distinct roles—live AI search/citation bots (which drive high-intent referral clicks) and foundation model training crawlers (which ingest datasets for pre-training).

CDN & WAF Double-Check

Even with a clean robots.txt, Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode or aggressive WAF rules sometimes challenge verified AI search bots with HTTP 403/429 responses. After updating your policy, inspect server access logs for OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot HTTP 200 hits, and pair your technical setup with live citation monitoring in Semrush AI Visibility or SE Ranking.
