Should I Block GPTBot but Allow OAI-SearchBot?

Updated November 2026 • 4-minute read • Quick GEO Answer

Short answer: Yes, for most commercial websites and publishers. If you want ChatGPT Search to cite your pages and send referral clicks, explicitly Allow OAI-SearchBot (and ChatGPT-User). If you want to opt out of foundation model training datasets, explicitly Disallow GPTBot. Name each OpenAI user-agent individually in robots.txt instead of using one blanket rule to block every AI crawler.

How OpenAI Splits Its Three Crawlers

A common technical GEO error is treating every OpenAI crawler as a single scraper. OpenAI separates search indexing, user-triggered browsing, and training collection across three distinct user-agents. Mixing them up—or blocking OpenAI IP ranges at the firewall level—silently removes your brand from ChatGPT Search citations.

4 Reproducible Steps to Verify Your Split Policy

  1. Build and publish /robots.txt: Generate your complete matrix (including Anthropic's Claude-SearchBot vs. ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot) in the robots.txt Policy Builder and deploy it at your root domain.
  2. Verify live HTTP 200 delivery: Fetch https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt in an incognito browser or with curl -I and confirm it returns 200 OK with text/plain content.
  3. Audit Cloudflare / WAF bot rules: Even a valid robots.txt fails if a CDN security rule issues HTTP 403 or JavaScript challenges to OAI-SearchBot. Check firewall events for OpenAI's published crawler IPs and allowlist verified search bots.
  4. Confirm real citations after indexing: A static robots file grants permission, not visibility. Once your crawler policy is clean, track whether ChatGPT Search and Google AI Overviews actually cite your target URLs over time using a multi-engine tracker such as SE Ranking AI Visibility.

When Should You Allow GPTBot Too?

Some early-stage B2B SaaS tools, open-source projects, and documentation hubs intentionally allow GPTBot as well as OAI-SearchBot. Their goal is broad brand recall inside future base models, not just live retrieval. Choose that "Open All AI" stance only if your public docs are free marketing assets and you have no licensing or paywall restrictions against training ingestion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I block GPTBot but allow OAI-SearchBot?

For most SaaS companies, agencies, and publishers, yes. Allowing OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User keeps your site eligible for live ChatGPT Search citations and referral clicks, while disallowing GPTBot opts your pages out of OpenAI foundation model training.

Will disallowing GPTBot remove my site from ChatGPT Search answers?

No, provided OAI-SearchBot is explicitly allowed in robots.txt and not blocked by your CDN or WAF. OpenAI documents GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot as separate user-agents with distinct roles.

Why should I avoid one blanket rule that blocks all AI bots?

Blanket AI blocks cut off live retrieval bots like OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Claude-SearchBot alongside training scrapers, eliminating high-intent citation traffic from AI search engines.
